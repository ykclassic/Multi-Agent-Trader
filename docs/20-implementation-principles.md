# Implementation Principles

1. Point-in-time correctness over convenience.
2. Deterministic calculations over LLM arithmetic.
3. Evidence over persuasive prose.
4. Abstention over hallucination.
5. Policy gates over model authority.
6. Append-only auditability over mutable histories.
7. Idempotency over optimistic retries.
8. Reconciliation over assumed state.
9. Research, backtest and live paths share domain contracts where practical.
10. Crypto and forex are first-class; stocks are out of scope.
11. No performance claim is accepted without reproducible, leakage-controlled evidence.
