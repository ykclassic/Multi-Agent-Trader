# Debate Protocol

## Goal
Produce evidence resolution, not persuasive prose.

## Bull/Bear
### Round 1 — independent thesis
Each side submits:
- thesis
- strongest evidence
- expected direction/horizon
- invalidation
- uncertainty

### Round 2 — attack
Each side identifies up to three weaknesses in the opposing case.

### Round 3 — evidence resolution
Resolver checks:
- freshness
- source reliability
- whether claims measure the same thing
- contradictions
- missing information

### Round 4 — synthesis
Research Manager outputs:
- consensus
- disagreements
- unknowns
- dominant risks
- evidence quality
- unresolved disagreement level

Do not force a consensus.

## Risk debate
Three bounded perspectives:
- Aggressive: identify opportunity cost/over-conservatism.
- Conservative: identify tail risks, invalidations and failure modes.
- Neutral: assess evidence and constraint compliance.

Risk debate cannot alter deterministic limits.

## Stop conditions
Debate ends after a configured maximum number of rounds, repeated claims, or sufficient evidence resolution. No unbounded agent conversation.
